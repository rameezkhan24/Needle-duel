Needle duel

Pop your friend's balloon head before they pop yours. Pull your string to lunge, lean back to dodge.

Online play: both players open this page on their own phone. One creates a room and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins with it. It connects phone to phone, so Wi-Fi works best. First to 3 pops wins.

Drag down fast to lunge. Drag up to lean back and dodge. Drag sideways to move. A full lunge needs a moment to recover.
